The Buy Box is the main Add-to-Cart area on a product page. When a shopper clicks it, the order goes to whoever is winning the Buy Box at that moment.

Most listings have multiple sellers offering the same product, but only one wins the Buy Box. Amazon picks based on price, stock, shipping speed, and seller performance. PPC students should know that winning the Buy Box is how the bulk of sales happen.

## Key terms

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Buy Box | The main Add-to-Cart button area on a product page |
| Buy Box winner | The single seller who receives the next order from that button |
