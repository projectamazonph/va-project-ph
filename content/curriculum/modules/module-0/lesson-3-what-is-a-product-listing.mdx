A product listing is the page on Amazon where one item is sold. It is the storefront that shoppers land on when they click a search result.

A good listing has a clear title, several photos, bullet points that describe features, a long description, and the price. Each listing also has an ASIN, which is Amazon's unique ID for that product.

## Key terms

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Listing | The single product page where shoppers decide to buy |
| ASIN | Amazon's unique product ID, used for ads and tracking |
