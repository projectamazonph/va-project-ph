Paid rank is the slot a seller buys through Amazon ads. These slots are also called sponsored placements or PPC, which stands for pay-per-click.

When a shopper clicks a sponsored product, the seller pays Amazon a small fee, and the listing lands closer to the top. Paid rank gives faster visibility than organic, but the placement stops the moment the seller stops paying.

## Key terms

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Sponsored product | A paid ad slot in Amazon search results |
| PPC | Pay-per-click, the pricing model Amazon ads use |
