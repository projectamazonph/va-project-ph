Organic rank is the free position a listing earns in Amazon search results. It is not paid for, which is why it is called organic, the way a plant grows without being pushed.

Amazon decides organic rank mostly by how well a listing matches the search, how often it sells, and how customers respond. Organic rank is slow to build but very valuable because every click from it is free.

## Key terms

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Organic result | A free listing position in search, earned by relevance and sales |
| Relevance | How well a listing matches the words a shopper typed |
