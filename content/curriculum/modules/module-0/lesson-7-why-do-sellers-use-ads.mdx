Sellers use ads because Amazon search is crowded, and organic rank alone can take months to build. Ads give a new listing a chance to be seen right away, even before it has a sales history.

Ads also produce useful data. Each campaign shows which keywords shoppers typed and which ones led to a sale. PPC students should think of ads both as a visibility tool and as a way to learn the market.

## Key terms

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Conversion | When a shopper who clicked actually buys |
| Keyword | The word or phrase a shopper typed that triggered an ad |
