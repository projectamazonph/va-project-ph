A seller account is the sign-up a person or company creates to start selling on Amazon. Once approved, the seller can add products and begin receiving orders.

There are two main kinds: Individual, for someone selling a few items, and Professional, for a brand or business selling in volume. Most PPC students will look at Professional accounts because that plan unlocks advertising tools.

## Key terms

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Individual account | Pay-per-item seller plan, best for low volume |
| Professional account | Monthly subscription plan, best for active sellers |
