# 39 - Simulator Research

Keyword Research Studio teaches a repeatable research workflow using synthetic source lists. It evaluates how a learner turns noisy terms into a structured, testable starting set.

## Case inputs

Cases contain a product brief, seed terms, customer-language notes, suggested and observed terms, volume or competition signals where available, and explicit data limitations. No case should require the learner to claim a keyword will convert.

## Task sequence

1. restate the product and customer intent;
2. group terms by intent and relevance;
3. remove duplicates, unsafe assumptions, or clearly irrelevant terms;
4. choose seed targets and a match-type starting point;
5. identify negative candidates and terms that need validation;
6. write the next research or testing step.

## Rubric

| Dimension | Full-credit behavior |
|---|---|
| Relevance | Keeps terms connected to the product and customer need |
| Intent grouping | Groups by a useful decision, not only by word similarity |
| Hygiene | Removes duplicates and documents uncertain terms |
| Match-type judgment | Chooses a starting control that fits the evidence |
| Next step | Names how the hypothesis will be checked |

## Safety and curriculum bridge

Research output is a practice artifact. It is not a promise of demand, rank, or sales. Link the attempt to the keyword-research lesson and, when a term is uncertain, suggest a search-term review rather than a blind launch. The mapping must remain aligned with [syllabus-to-tracks-reconciliation.md](./syllabus-to-tracks-reconciliation.md).

## Release requirements

Every dataset has an owner, source note, review date, and synthetic-data label. Expected groupings and negative rules are independently reviewed. A new research case is published as a new scenario version; old attempts remain readable.
