Version: 1.0 - Reviewed: 2026-08-17 - Owner: Product Owner + Growth lead (assign at GA)

The free-to-start, phone-first path that turns Filipino beginners into hired virtual assistants - with practice, not just videos.

## Content Engine

Weekly: 2 shorts + 1 Facebook post + 1 email.

Monthly: 1 deep case study (real student, verified, consented - feeds copy-bible testimonials).

Evergreen: free "VA Starter Kit" lead magnet (PDF from curriculum, branded, watermarked practice-safe).

Rule: every piece teaches one real thing; zero hype-only content (brand promise).

## Community Operations Handbook (condensed)

Spaces: FB Group (primary), Discord (study rooms), Messenger announcements.

House rules: no selling scams, no "guaranteed job" claims, kindness in Taglish/English, no sharing paid content.

Moderation: 2 mods minimum coverage PHT daytime; response SLA 12h; ban ladder warn to mute to ban (logged).

Program: weekly "Win Wednesday" (student wins), monthly live Q&A with a teacher, alumni channel for hired VAs (proof flywheel).

Safety: never DM-first outreach; all official offers only via app/email domains (anti-scam notice pinned - scam adjacency is real in this niche).

## SEO / Marketing Site Engineering

Next.js SSG for landing + blog; structured data (Organization, FAQPage, Course).

OG images generated per page (brand templates - future 31-brand-templates.md); Lighthouse at least 95 on landing.

Meta/copy from 35-copy-bible.md; all claims verifiable (no invented numbers - testimonial pipeline only).

Analytics: analytics gap file 23 events + UTM discipline (utm_source/channel/campaign registry maintained here).

## Guardrails

No growth mechanic may violate compliance-legal gap file 26 / 49-ph-compliance.md (privacy, consumer law) or brand promise.

Testimonials: written consent stored; real names/cities only with permission; results disclaimed ("individual results vary").

Paid ads (when used): disclose clearly; never target with false employment promises (Consumer Act, see 49-ph-compliance.md).

Scholarship announcements handled with Finance sign-off (see 46-billing-payments.md section on scholarship program).
