# 41 - Simulator Field Skills

Field-skill simulations connect separate PPC decisions into the work a VA must explain to a teammate or client.

## S8 - Campaign Architect

The learner turns a product brief and goal into a campaign map: campaign type, ad groups, targeting, match types, negatives, naming, budgets, and the first review plan. Grade structure and reasoning, not a single preferred layout.

## S9 - Account Audit

The learner reviews a synthetic account snapshot, identifies the highest-risk or highest-impact issue, ranks findings by confidence, and proposes a next step. The case must include missing evidence so the learner can practice asking for access or clarification.

## S11 - Client Onboarding

The learner turns an onboarding brief into a checklist: access boundaries, goals, product facts, constraints, reporting cadence, approval rules, and open questions. Real credentials are never requested in the simulator; the artifact teaches what should be confirmed before work begins.

## S12-S14 - Capstone sequence

These scenarios combine research, setup, optimization, reporting, and client communication around one synthetic practice account. The capstone is portfolio practice, not employment evidence. A teacher reviews the final rationale and checks that the student can explain uncertainty and safe next actions.

## Field-skill rubric

| Dimension | Full-credit behavior |
|---|---|
| Workflow | Connects tasks in a sensible order |
| Judgment | Prioritizes impact, evidence, and reversibility |
| Communication | Explains the work clearly to a client or teammate |
| Safety | Respects access, approval, and data boundaries |
| Reflection | Names what was learned and what needs practice |

Field-skill attempts require human review before being used for a certificate, intervention, or portfolio export. Calibration and appeals follow [50-teacher-quality.md](./50-teacher-quality.md).
