# Partnerships - Community, Education, Livelihood and Hiring

Version: 1.0 - Reviewed: 2026-08-17 - Owner: Growth lead + Product Owner (+ Finance for money terms)
Partnerships are distribution AND mission: the fastest honest path from "aspiring" to "hired" runs through BPO transition programs, schools, LGU livelihood offices, honest creators, and employers who hire our graduates.

## Partner Types & Value Exchange

| Partner | What they give | What we give | Tech |
|---|---|---|---|
| BPO transition programs | Cohorts of agents seeking WFH | Structured reskilling track + progress reporting | Partner Cohorts |
| Colleges / tech schools | Student access, credibility | Curriculum chapter (VA elective), certificates | Campus Tech |
| LGU livelihood / DOLE offices | Community reach, scholarship funding | Subsidized seats, localized onboarding | Livelihood Tech |
| Creators / affiliates | Trust-driven distribution | Rev-share on Pro/Cohorts, content kit | Creator Tech |
| Hiring partners (agencies, foreign SMEs) | Real jobs for graduates | Vetted, certified talent pipeline | Hiring Network |
| Scholarship sponsors (companies/OFW groups) | Funding | Named scholarship slots + impact reports | Scholar Fund (doc 46 section 8) |

## Tech Mechanics

Partner Cohorts
- Dedicated cohort instances (doc 05 teacher APIs), partner-branded welcome (brand rules, doc 31), shared progress dashboard for partner coordinator.
- Pricing: per-seat cohort rate; invoicing via doc 46 section 5 (withholding-ready).

Campus Tech
- 12-week chapter mapped to curriculum (Modules 0-10 + capstone); instructor kit = teacher training per doc 50.
- Students get Starter free; certification discounted; no grades-for-purchase - ever.

Creator Tech
- 20% rev-share first year on attributed Pro/Cohorts via tracked codes (doc 48 section 2 UTM registry).
- Vetting: content review for false promises (Consumer Act, doc 49 section 4); creators sign conduct addendum (no "guaranteed job" claims, no income screenshots without disclaimer).
- Kit: brand assets, copy do/don't (doc 35), fact sheet, disclosure templates.

Hiring Network
- Employers get access to opt-in certified graduates (students control visibility - consent recorded, doc 26).
- We never sell student data; employers see profile + certificate + capstone artifacts only.
- Feedback loop: employer skill feedback -> curriculum review (doc 22 section 6).

## Safeguards (non-negotiable)

- No pay-to-certify: certification standards identical for all students (doc 50); partners buy access, not outcomes.
- No job guarantees in any partner material; violations = tech suspension (clause in every agreement).
- Partner vetting checklist: legal registration, student treatment policy, no predatory lending tied to enrollment, refund alignment with doc 46 section 4.
- Scholarship selection documented and appealable (doc 46 section 8); no partner gets selection veto.
- Student consent required before any data crosses to a partner (cohort progress = aggregates unless explicit opt-in).

## Pipeline & Governance

Lead -> Intro call (GT) -> Pilot (1 cohort, discounted) -> Review vs section 5 metrics
-> MoU (FO legal template) -> Scale -> Quarterly partner review
Owners: GT runs pipeline; PO approves tech fit; FO approves money terms; SO approves data-sharing scope.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target (12 months) |
|---|---|
| Partner-sourced signups | 25% of total |
| Partner cohort activation vs organic parity | within 5pp |
| Partner student certification rate | at least organic minus 5pp |
| Graduate hire rate (Hiring Network, opt-ins) | at least 30% within 90 days of certification |
| Partner NPS | at least 40 |
| Creator-attributed revenue share accuracy disputes | 0 unresolved > 30 days |

## Documents Produced Per Partner

MoU template (FO), data-sharing addendum (SO), brand usage sheet (DL), one-pager per tech (template in doc 58 section 8), quarterly impact report (auto-generated aggregates + narrative).
